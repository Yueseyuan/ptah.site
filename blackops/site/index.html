// what we do

Exposure assessment for the whole attack surface

Every engagement starts with a signed scope of work — we test what you authorize, nothing else, and report what we find in plain language.

01

External Exposure Audit

What's actually visible to the internet: exposed devices, forgotten subdomains, default credentials, unpatched services indexed by internet-wide scanners.

02

OSINT & Digital Footprint Review

What public information exists about your organization or key personnel, and how it could be used against you — phishing pretext, credential stuffing, social engineering.

03

Cloud Configuration Audit

Privilege-escalation paths and misconfigurations across AWS, Azure, and GCP — mapped from your current access to what an attacker could ultimately reach.

04

Web Application Testing

Manual and automated testing of login flows, checkout paths, and APIs for the vulnerabilities that automated scanners alone tend to miss.

05

Vulnerability Scanning

Template-based scanning against thousands of known CVEs and misconfigurations, prioritized by real exploitability rather than raw CVSS score.

06

Active Directory Security Review

Attack-path mapping from a standard user account to domain admin — the same technique ransomware operators use, run defensively and reported to you first.

01

Scope & Authorize

We agree in writing on exactly what's being tested, the testing window, and who to contact if something needs to stop. Nothing runs before this is signed.

02

Reconnaissance

We map what's externally visible — assets, subdomains, exposed services, and public information tied to your organization.

03

Assessment

We test the in-scope systems for real, exploitable weaknesses — not just what a scanner flags, but what an attacker could actually use.

04

Report & Remediate

A plain-language report ranked by real-world risk, plus a walkthrough with your team on what to fix first and why.
